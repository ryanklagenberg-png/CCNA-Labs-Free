SCENARIO
Foxglen Public Library wired four floor-distribution switches in a ring for redundancy. All four uplinks are matching 1 Gbps fiber, so path costs alone won't decide everything. "Costs tie on the far side of the ring — you'll need to remember the BID tie-breaker rule to figure out which port goes into blocking. Walk me through it on the whiteboard before we cut over."
Mission: Identify Root Bridge, every Root Port, every Designated Port, and every Blocking Port. Watch for the tie-break.
